# Asset sources

Provenance for everything the game ships or builds at startup. All of it is
original work made for this project, except the bundled Noto fonts
(third party, SIL Open Font License). No image, model or sound files from
elsewhere are shipped, and no network service or asset store is used.

## Audio — original, generated

All 37 WAVs are generated by [`tools/gen_audio.py`](../tools/gen_audio.py)
(Python standard library only, fixed seed `1997`, deterministic): 16-bit
PCM, mono, 44.1 kHz. Every voice plays without panning or distance
attenuation (`src/audio.rs`); the whistle is never spatialized.

Regenerate: `python3 tools/gen_audio.py` (writes into `assets/audio/`).

## Textures and material maps — original, generated at runtime

`src/world/texture.rs` builds every surface texture at startup from the seed
(tileable value noise, strokes, weave patterns, stencilled lettering), each
with a box-filtered mip chain: ground grass/soil, road earth, weathered and
dark wood, rusted corrugated zinc, ceiba bark, foliage, burlap, straw, dark
and pale cloth, clay, water, the note paper, the 1998 calendar page, the
"HATO LA CEIBA" sign, nine painted location signs (original 5×7 stencil
glyphs) and the moon halo. Tangent-space normal maps (derived from each
albedo's luminance or a height field) and glTF-layout roughness/metallic
maps cover ground, road, wood, zinc, bark, burlap, straw, cloth and water.
`src/world/mesh.rs` supplies the tangent basis those maps need.

## Meshes and characters — original, generated at runtime

Everything is built procedurally from `Layout` data, so what is seen is what
collides (`src/world/`). No imported models.

| Kit | Source |
|---|---|
| Mesh toolkit: boxes, tubes, lathes, blobs, ribbons merged per material | `world/mesh.rs` |
| Ranch house, board by board, with door/window openings, zinc roof and porch | `world/house.rs` |
| Table lantern, note, shelf and jars, hammock, tinajas, bench, calendar, porch lamp | `world/props.rs` |
| The ceiba: buttress roots, fluted trunk, flat limbs, crown, altar hollow | `world/ceiba.rs` |
| Landmark kits: entry portal and gates, sheds, well, water tower, windmill and pump, shrine offerings, corral pens and shelter, fields shelter and cart, caño bridge, boardwalk and stilt hut, boat, watchtower ramp, deck, cabin and beacon, fences and signs | `world/district.rs` |
| Tall grass, reeds, cattails, lily pads and low water mist | `world/flora.rs` |
| Standing water, the ford and puddles following the terrain | `world/wet.rs` |
| Rain mesh, lightning flash and bolt | `world/weather.rs` |
| Ground, road, sky, moon, stars and moonlight | `world/land.rs` |
| Extraction truck and derelict ranch pickup | `world/vehicles.rs` |
| Zebu-type cattle with idle breathing and a nervous shudder | `world/herd.rs` |
| Bundles, peppers and wards, pings, lamp pools, power lamps, beacon fire, truck lamps | `world/dynamic.rs` |
| El Silbón: three-metre gaunt figure, broad woven sombrero hiding the face, ragged strips, long bony clawed hands, burlap femur sack; small joint hierarchy animated from the truth layer | `world/silbon.rs` |
| Teammates: llanero in a straw hat and party-coloured scarf and ruana, belt, boots, torch, carried bundle on the back | `world/avatar.rs` |

The Silbón and the shrine are a fictional, stylized interpretation of the
legend; nothing depicts real ritual practice. The eleven user-supplied
concept images were inspected only as authoring references (see
`docs/PROGRESS.md`); their licensing was not established and they are not
copied or shipped.
