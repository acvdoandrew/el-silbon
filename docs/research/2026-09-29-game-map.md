EL SILBÓN: THE RETURN — MAP OF THE CURRENT GAME, FOR DESIGNERS (as of 3288232)

HARD CONSTRAINTS (any new idea has to respect these)
- The whistle is inverted and never placed in space. Faint means he is under ~16 m away, middling ~16–28 m, loud over ~28 m. It never tells you his direction.
- Omens and phantoms appear near the listener and never reveal where he really is. Tureco the dog is the only cue that tells the truth, and only up close.
- In-game proximity voice is on hold (AGENTS.md; roadmap item B11 is the user's call). The friends play over Discord-style voice across Tailscale, so any voice comedy has to come from the game creating situations, not from in-game audio.
- These rules are enforced by tests: his hunt is always slower than a walking player, and a warning always lasts more than 1.5 s.
- Everything comes from the night's seed. The host decides every outcome.

MAP AND LANDMARKS (about 110 × 120 m; walking 3.6 m/s, so each leg takes about 20–35 s)
- Entrada (gate, where you start)
- El Rancho (house: bundle on the table, notes, shelf radio; Tureco tied behind)
- El Molino (windmill: pump crank, lamp panel, padlocked key box)
- El Corral (the herd)
- La Ceiba (tree with the altar in its roots)
- El Pajonal (tall-grass fields)
- El Caño (stream and stilt hut)
- El Mirador (lookout tower with a beacon; open deck he can see from everywhere)
- La Ciénaga (marsh)
- La Salida (truck, 7 m boarding zone)

THE LOOP OF A NIGHT
1. Five bone bundles are hidden at: the house table (always there), the corral (3 possible spots), the fields (3), the stilt hut (2) and the lookout (2). The first pickup wakes him. He rises at least 30 m from everyone, on the far side of his patrol, and cannot warn anyone for his first 9 s. Before that, a distant whistle sounds every 28–46 s.
2. Carry bundles to the ceiba. You can carry several, but each one costs 6% speed (never below 62%) and adds 1.6 m of rattle noise. Hold at the altar for 3 s per bundle, with skill checks during it.
3. Once all five are home, crank the windmill pump for 10 s. Players' cranking adds together. It makes a 34 m noise every 1.5 s. The dynamo can light only 2 of 3 lamp lines (hacienda, corral, bridge); the bridge starts dark and the panel switches which pair is on.
4. Open the key box by dialling 3 digits (new every night). The digits are in 3 of the 20 pages: Madrina's note at the ceiba, the foreman's ledger in the stilt hut, the guard's log in the lookout cabin. A wrong code rattles (12 m noise).
5. Hold the ignition for 6 s, with skill checks. It makes a 75 m noise every second. Once the engine starts, pressure goes to maximum and he comes straight at you. It needs 26 s to warm up. You win when every standing player is in the zone and nobody is downed.
6. The other way to win: with all bones home, press N at the altar and name tonight's version of him. Right name: banished. Wrong name: he comes furious, and you can't try again for 60 s.
- Solo, being caught ends the run. In co-op, the run fails when nobody is standing.
- Side systems: 20 lore pages, counted across nights in the Journal. 7 ají pickups, carry up to 3. 6 spare battery packs.

RUN LENGTH
- No human run has been timed.
- A test requires the full scripted solo route to take 3–14 min.
- The rendered test route (one won night plus one caught night) is 586 s of simulated time.
- The night's pressure reaches its worst at 840 s (14 min).
- The "quick hands" distinction goes to a win under 8 min.

HIS BEHAVIOUR (Normal difficulty)
- States:
  - Dormant
  - Stalking: walks the trail network at 2.2 m/s, waits about 13 m from a player he can't see, creeps toward a visible player at 1.2 m/s, investigates noises (remembers them 14 s, searches 6 s), roams the whole map after 20 s of patience.
  - Warning: he sees you within 22 m. You have 3.5 s, and breaking line of sight for 0.75 s averts it. After 3 averts in a row he sinks and rises far away (2 on Gentle, 4 on Hard).
  - Hunting: moves at 2.3 m/s. If he keeps you in sight for 7 s you go down (up to 1.8× faster at point blank), or at 1.5 m he catches you. Your built-up exposure drops 0.35/s while hidden, and after 2.5 s out of sight he loses track, sinks and rises far away.
  - Counting (stopped by ají)
  - Hauling (carrying someone off in his sack)
- He picks the nearest visible player, but a player counts as 10 m closer per bundle carried and 8 m closer per unit of fear. He goes for carriers and frightened players.
- Pressure = 0.12 + 0.5 × (time / 840 s) + 0.07 per bundle carried + 0.08 per bundle laid (×1.25 for the Son, ×1.3 on Hard, ×0.5 on Gentle), capped at 0.9, and 1.0 once the engine runs. At full pressure:
  - stalk 3.2 m/s, creep 1.8, hunt 3.1 (capped at 0.9 × walking speed)
  - notices you from 27.5 m, warning shortens to 2.6 s, exposure to 4.9 s
  - cooldowns and patience halved, hears 1.6× farther
  - whistles up to 35% more often
- Whistle rhythm while stalking: usually 5–11 s apart; 15% of the time an "answer" 1.8–3.4 s later; 20% of the time silence for 15–26 s. Every 3.2 s while warning and 3.8 s while hunting (±35%). Four recorded takes, never the same one twice in a row.

STEALTH AND NOISE (how far each sound carries to him, in metres)
- Footsteps: crouch 2.5, walk 8, sprint 22. Wading adds 8, planks add 6.
- Actions: pickup 5, drop 11, ají 7, revive 4, altar 22, susto scream 34, missed check 40, cattle stampede 48, dog bark 30, beacon 95 (every 0.5 s).
- Rain shrinks every noise by up to 32% and thunder by 30% more (never below 30% of normal). Lightning comes in 26 s slots.
- How far he sees you: crouching ×0.6, torch on ×1.3. Crouched in tall grass, he can't see you beyond 5 m.
- Movement: walk 3.6 m/s, crouch 1.8, sprint 5.2, wading ×0.55, crawl 0.9 when downed. Sprint stamina lasts about 4 s and comes back 1.1 s after you stop.

FEAR AND SUSTO (fear runs 0–1)
- Rises per second: dark +0.008; dark and no teammate within 9 m (co-op only) +0.012 more; +0.003 per bundle carried; being hunted up to +0.14.
- Falls per second: teammate near −0.05, lamplight −0.06, praying (5 s hold at an empty altar, makes noise) −0.22 and then resets to 0.
- One-off jumps: he warns you +0.12, faint whistle +0.05, middling whistle +0.02, your own missed check +0.12.
- At 1.0 you get a susto: frozen for 1.1 s, a 34 m scream, fear drops back to 0.55.
- At fear ≥0.55: phantom glimpses of him, footsteps behind you that are nobody's, and (co-op only) false teammate marks.
- At fear ≥0.7: phantom whistles, about one every 25 s.

TORCH
- 300 s of battery (Hard 210, Gentle 450). A spare pack gives back 60%; you can't take one above 95%. Below 18% the beam gutters.
- He can see a lit torch from 45 m, and it pulls his attention every 2 s. The dark is safer but makes you more scared.

AJÍ
- Thrown 1.6 m in front of you. Makes a 3.4 m zone that lasts 24 s.
- The first time he touches it he stops and counts bones for 7.5 s (×1.3 for the Son), deaf and blind. After that the zone is a wall he won't walk into.

TURECO (the dog)
- Untie him behind the house (1.5 s hold). He follows whoever freed him, 1.8 m behind, at up to 5.5 m/s.
- He growls to his friend when the Silbón is really within 22 m.
- Within 7 m, if his courage has come back (90 s), he barks: the Silbón sinks away and rises far off, and drops anyone in his sack.

SKILL CHECKS (laying bones, cranking the pump, turning the ignition)
- One every 2.5–5 s of work; at the altar the first comes 0.5 s in.
- A 0.6 s chime, then the needle crosses once in 1.1 s. The target zone is 14% of the track (placed at 45–82%). Hitting its first 4% is "great".
- Great: +4% on the pump or truck, +12% on the rite hold.
- Miss or no press: a 40 m screech everyone hears, +0.12 fear, −8% on the pump or truck, or the current bundle's 3 s altar hold starts over.
- Hard shrinks the zone to ×0.75; Gentle widens it to ×1.4.

BEACON (at the lookout)
- A 2 s hold. It burns 26 s, pulses a 95 m noise at the tower that draws him there, then needs 70 s to cool. A deliberate lure.

CATTLE
- Walking or sprinting within 10 m of the herd without crouching unsettles it (bundles make it worse). When it breaks: a 48 m bellow, then a 20 s cooldown.

OMENS (one player at a time)
- Need quiet: first after 40–70 s, then every 35–70 s, shorter as pressure rises (70% of that on Hard); never while he is chasing you.
- Before he wakes: silence, distant bones.
- Once he is awake: lamps dying, drag marks, his hat on the trail.
- Late in the night: a torch far off that belongs to nobody.

THE THREE VERSIONS OF HIM (from the seed, revealed after the run)
- Borracho (the Drunkard): hears 1.4× farther, walks 8% slower, slurred whistle, bottles clink every 60–110 s.
- Hijo (the Son): weeps each time a bundle is laid, each bundle angers him 1.25× more, counts ají 1.3× longer.
- Arriero (the Drover): walks 6% faster, the cattle bellow when he is within 18 m of the herd, whip cracks every 60–110 s.

DOWNED, THE SACK, DEATH
- Downed: you drop everything, crawl, and bleed out in 60 s. A teammate revives you with a 4 s hold; that progress drains at half speed if they let go. Revived players come back at fear 0.5 with full stamina.
- If he downs someone while others are still standing, he puts them in his sack and walks at 1.7 m/s toward the point farthest from the standing players. After 35 s, or when he arrives, they are gone (dead).
- Ají in his path or Tureco's bark makes him drop them. The 60 s bleed-out keeps running while you are in the sack.
- Dead players can do nothing for the rest of the run: no spectating, no pings, no actions.

WHAT CO-OP PLAYERS CAN DO TO AND FOR EACH OTHER TODAY
- Help:
  - revive; free a sacked friend with ají or Tureco
  - hand bundles over (G to put down, E to pick up)
  - crank the pump together
  - calm each other by staying within 9 m
  - pings (V): one active per player, 14 s, 140 m, 2 s cooldown, silent to him; downed players can still ping
  - light the beacon or use noise to pull him away from someone
  - hear each other's footsteps as placed sounds
- Things that tend to cause blame and laughs:
  - your susto scream (34 m) or a missed check (40 m) draws him to everyone
  - stampedes (credited to whoever stirred the herd)
  - sprinting noise, and whoever carries the most bones becomes his target
  - false marks at high fear
  - drive off (X): anyone aboard the ready truck can leave now; everyone else gets "They left without you." This is also the only way to escape while a teammate is downed.
- End-of-night awards, up to 2 per player: Left behind, Rode in his sack, First to fall, Screamed the most, Butterfingers, His favourite, Guardian angel, Bone bearer, Ají in every pocket, Started a stampede, Tureco's friend, Untouched.
- Distinctions: silent as the grass, unbroken, nobody left behind, the one who named him, quick hands, keeper of the tale.
- The four survivors (Llanero, Coplera, Encargado, Muchacho) are cosmetic only.
- Difficulty: Gentle, Normal or Hard. Solo gets a new seed every launch; co-op uses the host's seed.

PLAYTEST NOTES VS. WHAT THE CODE ALREADY HAS
- Fullscreen and brightness: both already exist in Settings (fullscreen toggle, brightness −1 to +1). This looks like a discoverability problem or a Windows bug. Contrast really is missing.
- "Rising difficulty": already built (+0.08 pressure per bundle laid, plus carried bundles and time). The issue is that players don't feel it.
- Missed skill check "didn't slow progress": the penalty is real but small. On the pump, −8% is only about 0.8 s of the 10 s crank.
- Whistle sounds the same: there are only three distance bands (faint under 16 m, middling 16–28, loud over 28), and the whistle is now a third-party recording.
- Truck code: 3 of the 20 pages hold the digits.
- Downed allies hard to find: today there is only a pinged mark and the crawl.
