# El Silbón — The Return: trailer 2 (EN / ES)

One picture, two languages. The cut, the shots and the sound are shared;
the narration, the cards and the subtitles switch with `--lang`.
Narration is one llanero voice telling the tale (see `SOURCES.md`).

Rules for this cut: no tool or AI credits on screen; no store call to
action (Steam is only a maybe); only what the game really does — no dawn
sky (dawn is a card and its sound), no spectator view (there is none to
film), the veil is him gone while the whistle goes on.

## Narration

| id | English | Español |
|---|---|---|
| vo1 | Out on the llano, when it rains at night… nobody goes outside. | En el llano, cuando llueve de noche… nadie sale. |
| vo2 | They say a boy killed his own father. | Dicen que un muchacho mató a su propio padre. |
| vo3 | And his grandfather cursed him to carry the bones in a sack… forever. | Y el abuelo lo maldijo a cargar los huesos en un saco… para siempre. |
| vo4 | And to whistle, so everyone would know he was coming. | Y a silbar, para que todos sepan que viene. |
| vo5 | Every bone you bring home… angers him more. | Cada hueso que vuelve a la ceiba… lo enfurece más. |
| vo6 | Sometimes you won't see him at all. You will only hear him. | A veces no lo vas a ver. Solo lo vas a oír. |
| vo7 | If you hear him close… he is far away. | Si lo oyes cerca… está lejos. |
| vo8 | But if you hear him far away… | Pero si lo oyes lejos… |
| vo9 | …he's already here. | …ya está aquí. |

vo4 and vo5 are the Madrina's own chapters (IV, *La maldición*); vo7–vo9
are the game's rule, *el silbido miente*.
