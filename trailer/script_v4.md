# El Silbón — The Return: three trailers (EN / ES), 2026-10-01

Three cuts that differ in kind, not just in order. Each is EN and ES (the
cards and subtitles switch with `--lang`). Rules as before: no tool or AI
credits, no store call to action, only what the game really does, no
whooshes on the cuts, no synthesized bones rattle or groans for comedy.
Edits: `trailer/cut4.py --cut radio|coop|teaser --lang en|es` (shared
machinery in `trailer/cutkit.py`). Shots: `src/trailer.rs`.

---

## A. «La Voz del Llano» — the broadcast (about 100 s)

The tale comes out of the shelf radio in the dark house: *Cuentos de
camino* on La Voz del Llano, 11 p.m., November 1963 (`lore.rs` page 10).
The host reads a listener's letter, the static takes it, a news bulletin
breaks in (the clipping and the telegram: Santa Rosa, the flooded road,
the truck), then the host comes back, closer, to the ones still out there.
His last line is cut off by the static — the card finishes it.

Voice: one designed voice, an older llanero radio host; through an AM
radio filter on the tale and the bulletin, dry and close on the last
lines.

## B. «Nadie se queda atrás» / «No One Left Behind» — co-op (about 75 s)

No narrator. Bold cards on the beat of a driving joropo-horror score (harp,
cuatro, maracas, drums, low strings), the game's own sounds under every
shot. It shows the mechanics: four friends, his bones, the ceiba, La
Rabia, pepper wards, Tureco, the tower's fire, the pump and the truck, a
friend down crawling and a friend going back, the whistle that lies, the
catch.
